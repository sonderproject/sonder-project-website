AI consulting FAQ

What does an AI consultant do for a small business?

We find the repetitive work eating your team’s time, decide where AI genuinely pays off, then build and set up the automations and tools — and train your team to use them.

Do you offer AI consulting in San Diego?

Yes. Sonder Consulting works with businesses in San Diego in person or remotely, and with companies anywhere in the US.

How much does AI consulting cost?

The first 30-minute call is free. After that, every engagement is scoped to a defined outcome with a fixed proposal, so you know the cost before work starts.

What kinds of things can you automate?

Lead follow-up, intake and scheduling, document and email drafting, reporting, data entry between apps, customer support answers and internal knowledge search, among others.

Do I need technical staff to use what you build?

No. We build around the tools you already use and hand over simple, documented workflows your team can run day to day.

Which AI tools do you work with?

We pick the right model and tools for the job — including OpenAI and Anthropic models and common automation platforms — rather than forcing one vendor.
