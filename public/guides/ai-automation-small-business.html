7 things a small business can automate with AI

By Sonder Project · San Diego · Updated November 2026 · 6 min read

You don’t need a data science team to get value from AI. The best first projects are boring, repetitive tasks your team does every day. Here are seven we see pay off again and again.

1. Lead follow-up

Reply to every new inquiry within minutes with a personalized message, then remind your team to call.

2. Intake and qualification

Turn a form or a call transcript into a structured record and a priority, so the best leads get attention first.

3. Scheduling

Let customers book, reschedule and get reminders without back-and-forth.

4. Drafting emails and documents

Proposals, follow-ups and summaries drafted from your notes, then reviewed by a person.

5. Reporting

Weekly numbers pulled together automatically instead of copied from three systems.

6. Answering common questions

An assistant trained on your services and policies handles the questions you answer every day.

7. Data entry between apps

When a sale, booking or form comes in, update every system it touches automatically.

Where to start

List the tasks your team repeats every week, estimate the hours, and automate the biggest one first. We built Surfdesk on the same idea: say one sentence and it files the note, sets the reminder and counts the result.

FAQ

Is AI automation expensive for a small business?

It doesn't have to be. Many automations run on tools you already pay for, and the right first project often saves hours a week. Start with one repetitive task and measure the time saved.

Will AI replace my staff?

The best small-business automations remove busywork — copying data, drafting the same email, chasing follow-ups — so your team spends more time with customers.
