Surfdesk FAQ

What is Surfdesk?

Surfdesk is a client tracker that keeps follow-ups from slipping. Say it once — “Marcus got the job, call him Friday” — and it saves the note, sets the reminder and counts the outcome. Every client, follow-up and visit lives in one place, on any phone.

Who is Surfdesk for?

Nonprofits, social-service agencies and case-management teams use it as a shared caseload with outcome tracking and funder-ready Impact reports. Small businesses, freelancers and solo professionals use it to keep every client, service and follow-up in one place.

How much does Surfdesk cost?

Individual is $9 a month with a 7-day free trial and no card needed. Small Team is $19 per user per month for 2–18 people. Organization is $349 a month for 19 or more, with 20 seats included and extra seats at $15. Enterprise pricing is custom. Team plans include a 14-day free trial.

Can Surfdesk build funder reports?

Yes. Surfdesk counts people served and outcomes reached as your team works, and turns them into a polished Impact report PDF in one click — no end-of-month spreadsheet scramble.

Does Surfdesk work on my phone?

Yes. Surfdesk runs in any modern browser on iPhone, Android and desktop, and you can add it to your home screen like an app.

Is there a discount for skipping the trial?

Yes. If you'd rather start right away, you get 30% off your first month.
