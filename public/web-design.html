Web design FAQ

Do you build websites for real estate agents in San Diego?

Yes. Sonder Studio designs and builds custom websites for real estate agents, teams and brokerages in San Diego and across California, and works remotely with clients nationwide.

How much does a custom website cost?

Every site is scoped to your goals, so pricing is custom. The free 30-minute call is where we map what you need and give you a clear proposal with a fixed scope and timeline.

Do you use templates?

No. Every site is designed from scratch around your brand, market and audience. We use AI tools to move fast, but a designer makes every decision.

How long does a website take?

Most projects launch in a few weeks, depending on scope. You get a defined timeline in your proposal before any work starts.

Can you add AI features to my website?

Yes. We integrate AI where it helps conversion — lead capture and qualification, chat assistants trained on your listings or services, and automated follow-up.

Is the site optimized for Google?

Yes. Every build ships with fast load times, clean semantic markup, structured data, meta tags and a sitemap, so search engines understand and rank it.
