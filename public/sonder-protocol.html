Sonder Protocol FAQ

What is Sonder Protocol?

Sonder Protocol publishes practical household preparedness guides. Protocol 001 is The 30-Day Blackout Playbook, a plan for power outages.

What's in The 30-Day Blackout Playbook?

One 42-page household plan: what to buy, how much, and what to do hour by hour when the power goes out — water, light, radios, power, heat and CO safety, food, first aid and sanitation.

How do I get it?

It’s an instant PDF download from the Sonder Protocol site.
